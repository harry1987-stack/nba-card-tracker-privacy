# NBA Card Tracker — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 October 2026**

## Overview

NBA Card Tracker is a personal application used to manage a private basketball-card collection and investment portfolio.

## Information accessed

With the account owner's authorisation, NBA Card Tracker may access eBay order and purchase details, including item titles, item identifiers, purchase dates, prices, postage charges, seller details, and order or delivery statuses.

The application may also process basketball-card listing information to identify players, card sets, parallels, serial numbers, and other collecting attributes.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Maintain purchase and ownership records.
- Reconcile purchased cards against existing portfolio records.
- Track expenditure and delivery status.
- Identify duplicate records and missing transaction information.
- Support personal basketball-card investment analysis.

## Storage and security

Purchase records may be stored in the account owner's private Google Sheets, local computer, and locally hosted automation systems.

Authentication credentials and access tokens are not intentionally published in the public GitHub repository or shared with the local AI model.

## Information sharing

The application does not sell personal information or intentionally publish private purchase records.

Information may be processed by connected service providers, including eBay, Google, and relevant automation infrastructure, as necessary for the application's functions.

## Data retention and deletion

Purchase and portfolio records are retained for personal recordkeeping until deleted by the account owner.

The account owner can revoke eBay authorisation and remove locally stored records.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
